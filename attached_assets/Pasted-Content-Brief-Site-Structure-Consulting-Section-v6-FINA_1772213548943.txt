I have spent my career solving hard problems in environments where failure is not an option. As an Adjunct Professor at Indiana Wesleyan University, a board member for the Mid-South ACME, and an active participant in the CCME, my focus is on building the practical, auditable governance frameworks that organizations need to thrive in the age of AI.
