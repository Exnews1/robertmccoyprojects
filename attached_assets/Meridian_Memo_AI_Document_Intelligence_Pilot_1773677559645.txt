I am writing to inform you that the Knowledge Management Office, in
partnership with our Information Technology division, will be launching
a pilot program to evaluate AI-assisted document classification and
ingestion beginning April 1, 2025.

BACKGROUND

Over the past eighteen months, our document intake volume has increased
by approximately 40 percent, placing significant strain on the manual
review process. The average time from document submission to repository
availability is currently 3.2 business days, with classification
accuracy at 87 percent on first review.

The AI-Assisted Document Intelligence pilot will introduce automated
classification capabilities to the intake workflow. Human reviewers
will retain full authority to approve, modify, or reject all
classifications. No document will enter the Knowledge Repository
without human approval.

PILOT SCOPE

  - Duration: April 1 through June 30, 2025 (90 days)
  - Participating Divisions: Operations, Engineering, Human Resources
  - Volume Target: Minimum 500 documents processed
  - Success Metrics: Classification accuracy above 92 percent,
    processing time under 1 business day

GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS

All AI classification decisions are logged in the audit trail with
the method tagged as AI-assisted. Confidence scores below 70 percent
are flagged for priority human review. Weekly accuracy reports will
be distributed to participating division heads.

S. Park
Chief Data Officer
