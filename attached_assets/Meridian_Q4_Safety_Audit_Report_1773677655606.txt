Quarterly Safety Audit Report: Q4 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Health and Safety Division completed quarterly safety audits
across all four Meridian Industrial Group facilities during Q4 2024.
A total of 127 inspection points were evaluated across manufacturing,
warehousing, office, and laboratory environments. Overall compliance
improved to 94.3 percent, up from 91.7 percent in Q3 2024.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDINGS BY FACILITY

Facility A - Manufacturing Plant (Columbus)
  Inspection Points: 42
  Compliance Rate: 93.1%
  Critical Findings: 0
  Non-Critical Findings: 3
  - Two emergency exit signs with burned-out illumination (corrected)
  - One fire extinguisher past inspection date (replaced)
  - Forklift traffic lane markings faded in Aisle 7 (scheduled)

Facility B - Distribution Center (Indianapolis)
  Inspection Points: 35
  Compliance Rate: 97.1%
  Critical Findings: 0
  Non-Critical Findings: 1
  - Loading dock safety chain missing from Bay 4 (corrected)

Facility C - Corporate Office (Chicago)
  Inspection Points: 28
  Compliance Rate: 96.4%
  Critical Findings: 0
  Non-Critical Findings: 1
  - AED unit on Floor 3 past battery replacement date (corrected)

Facility D - Research Laboratory (Ann Arbor)
  Inspection Points: 22
  Compliance Rate: 90.9%
  Critical Findings: 0
  Non-Critical Findings: 2
  - Chemical storage cabinet exceeded capacity in Lab 201 (corrected)
  - Eye wash station flow test overdue in Lab 105 (corrected)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

INCIDENT SUMMARY

Total Recordable Incidents (Q4): 3
  - Slip and fall in Facility A parking lot (November 2): 2 lost days
  - Hand laceration in Facility A packaging area (November 19): 0 lost days
  - Ergonomic strain in Facility C (December 8): 1 lost day

Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR): 1.47 (industry average: 3.1)
Days Away, Restricted, or Transferred (DART): 0.73
Near-Miss Reports Submitted: 24 (up from 18 in Q3)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Implement monthly inspection of all emergency lighting systems
   to prevent recurrence of illumination failures.
2. Increase near-miss reporting incentive program to sustain the
   positive trend in proactive hazard identification.
3. Schedule ergonomic assessments for all corporate office
   workstations by Q2 2025.
4. Update chemical storage capacity signage in research laboratory
   to improve self-monitoring by lab personnel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

CONCLUSION

Overall safety performance in Q4 2024 was strong, with continued
improvement in compliance rates and below-industry-average incident
rates. No critical findings were identified. All non-critical
findings have been corrected or have scheduled remediation dates.
The Health and Safety Division will continue quarterly audits in
Q1 2025 with a focus on ergonomic risk assessment.
