TERMINOLOGY

Append-Only Log: A data structure that permits adding new records
but prohibits modification or deletion of existing records. Used
for audit trails to ensure data integrity and non-repudiation.

Classification Confidence: A numerical score (0 to 1.0) indicating
the system's certainty in its document type assignment. Scores
above 0.85 are considered high confidence.

Data Steward: A designated individual responsible for implementing
data governance standards within a specific division or domain.

Document Intelligence Engine: The AI-assisted system that automates
document classification, standardized naming, and metadata
extraction during the knowledge repository ingestion process.

Human-In-The-Loop (HITL): A governance model requiring human review
and approval before AI-generated recommendations are enacted.
Ensures accountability and prevents autonomous system actions.

Knowledge Repository: The centralized, governed document library
maintained by Meridian Industrial Group. All documents in the
repository have been classified, reviewed, and approved through
the standard ingestion workflow.

Legal Hold: A directive to preserve all records and documents
related to reasonably anticipated litigation, preventing routine
destruction under retention schedules.

Staging Queue: The interim holding area where documents await
human review after automated classification. Documents remain
in the staging queue until explicitly approved, modified, or
rejected by an authorized reviewer.

Standard Name: The normalized document filename assigned by the
rules engine during ingestion, following the pattern
[ORG]-[TYPE]-[SUBJECT]-[YYYY]-[SEQ].
