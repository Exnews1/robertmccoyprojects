Speaker Biography – Robert McCoy

Robert McCoy is a lifelong servant-leader, aerospace executive, and education advocate whose career spans over five decades across military, engineering, and community leadership. With a foundation forged in both the United States Army and Navy, Robert has built a legacy of mission-driven leadership, technical excellence, and a deep commitment to empowering others through education and second chances.

He served over 21 years in the U.S. military, including nine and a half years on active duty, holding roles as a Sonar Technician in the Navy and later as a Chief Warrant Officer and Aviator in the Army. His assignments included work on Presidential Support Staff, and his military experience continues to inform his disciplined, values-based leadership approach.

Following his military service, Robert transitioned into the aerospace and defense sector, where he has contributed to some of the most complex and impactful engineering efforts of the modern era. His leadership in programs like the F-35 Joint Strike Fighter and NASA’s Martian Lander reflect not only technical acumen but also the ability to lead diverse teams through high-stakes, high-complexity missions. As Director of Operations for Belcan Corporation, he oversaw more than $42 million in engineering programs, scaling global teams and improving technical delivery through ISO-based quality systems.

Today, Robert serves as the Military Partnership Executive at Indiana Wesleyan University, where he builds strategic education and workforce initiatives that connect veterans with high-demand careers in STEM, aerospace, and emerging technologies. He holds an MBA, a Bachelor of Science in Aeronautics, and has recently completed a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics.

Beyond his professional roles, Robert is a passionate community servant. He volunteers in prison ministry and teaches in Indiana’s correctional system, where he mentors incarcerated individuals and promotes education as a pathway to dignity and purpose. He also serves on the board of the MidSouth Advisory Council on Military Education.

Robert’s voice is one of uncommon depth — a rare fusion of military discipline, technical insight, and unshakable moral conviction. Whether speaking on leadership, AI ethics, veteran reintegration, or redemption through education, Robert McCoy delivers a message that is as real as it is relevant.
