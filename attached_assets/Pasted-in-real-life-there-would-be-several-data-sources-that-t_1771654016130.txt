Yes — and mapping those data sources is one of the most important things you can do for both credibility and architecture clarity.

What you’re really building is not “an AI tool.”

You’re building a data binding layer across fragmented institutional systems.

That distinction is huge — and audiences (especially DoD, ESO, education leaders) will recognize it immediately if you show it clearly.

Below is a practical, real-world data source map you can use for CMGF.

1. The Core Principle

In real deployment, the AI would not “pull data.”

The system would receive signals from authoritative systems.

So your architecture should say:

Authoritative Systems → CMGF Binding Layer → AI Explanation Layer

That keeps governance clean.
