SUBJECT:  Launch of AI-Assisted Document Intelligence Pilot Program

I am writing to inform you that the Knowledge Management Office, in
partnership with our Information Technology division, will be launching
a pilot program to evaluate AI-assisted document classification and
ingestion beginning April 1, 2025.

BACKGROUND

Over the past eighteen months, our document intake volume has increased
by approximately 40%, placing significant strain on the manual review
process. The average time from document submission to repository
availability is currently 3.2 business days, with classification
accuracy at 87% on first review. These metrics, while acceptable, do
not scale to projected growth.

The AI-Assisted Document Intelligence pilot will introduce automated
classification capabilities to the intake workflow. The system will
analyze document content and assign a recommended document type,
subject descriptor, and confidence score. Human reviewers will retain
full authority to approve, modify, or reject all classifications. No
document will enter the Knowledge Repository without human approval.

PILOT SCOPE

The pilot will operate under the following parameters:

  - Duration: April 1 through June 30, 2025 (90 days)
  - Participating Divisions: Operations, Engineering, and Human Resources
  - Document Types: All types in the current taxonomy
  - Volume Target: Minimum 500 documents processed through the
    AI-assisted pipeline
  - Success Metrics: Classification accuracy above 92%, average
    processing time reduced to under 1 business day, reviewer
    satisfaction score above 4.0 out of 5.0

GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS

This pilot operates under existing data governance policies. The
following additional controls apply:

  - All AI classification decisions are logged in the audit trail
    with the method tagged as "AI-assisted" to distinguish from
    manual classifications.
  - The classification service operates as a recommendation engine
    only. It does not have write access to the Knowledge Repository.
  - Confidence scores below 70% will be flagged for priority human
    review rather than presented as standard recommendations.
  - Weekly accuracy reports will be generated and distributed to
    participating division heads and the Data Governance Council.
  - An independent review of the pilot will be conducted at the
    30-day and 60-day marks.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOUR TEAMS

For Data Stewards in participating divisions: You will notice a new
"AI Classification" panel in the staging queue interface showing the
system's recommended classification alongside the confidence score.
Your review process remains the same. You approve, modify, or reject
as you normally would. The only difference is that the system now
provides a starting recommendation rather than presenting a blank
classification form.

For Division Heads: You will receive weekly summary reports showing
classification volumes, accuracy rates, and processing times for
your division. Please share any concerns or observations with the
Knowledge Management Office promptly so we can adjust the pilot
parameters as needed.

Training sessions for participating reviewers will be held during
the week of March 24. Calendar invitations will follow separately.

QUESTIONS AND FEEDBACK

Direct all questions about the pilot to the Knowledge Management
Office at km-support@meridian-ig.com or contact me directly. Your
feedback during this pilot is essential to determining whether
AI-assisted classification becomes a permanent part of our
document management infrastructure.

Thank you for your continued commitment to maintaining the quality
and accessibility of Meridian's organizational knowledge.

S. Park
Chief Data Officer
Meridian Industrial Group
