This memorandum outlines the mandatory training requirements for
calendar year 2025. All employees must complete the designated
training modules by the specified deadlines. Division heads and
supervisors are responsible for ensuring their teams achieve 100
percent compliance.

2025 MANDATORY TRAINING MODULES

Module 1: Workplace Safety Orientation Refresher
  Deadline: March 31, 2025
  Duration: 45 minutes
  Applies to: All employees

Module 2: Cybersecurity Awareness
  Deadline: March 31, 2025
  Duration: 60 minutes
  Applies to: All employees with network access

Module 3: Anti-Harassment and Workplace Conduct
  Deadline: June 30, 2025
  Duration: 90 minutes
  Applies to: All employees (supervisors require extended module)

Module 4: Data Governance and Records Management
  Deadline: June 30, 2025
  Duration: 30 minutes
  Applies to: All employees

Module 5: Emergency Response and Evacuation
  Deadline: September 30, 2025
  Duration: 30 minutes
  Applies to: All employees

COMPLIANCE TRACKING

Training completion is tracked through the learning management
system. Supervisors can view team compliance dashboards at any
time. Monthly compliance reports will be distributed to division
heads on the first business day of each month.

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE

Employees who fail to complete mandatory training by the specified
deadline will have their non-compliance noted in their performance
record. Continued non-compliance beyond 30 days past the deadline
may result in temporary suspension of system access until training
is completed. Supervisors with team compliance below 90 percent
at any deadline will be required to submit a corrective action
plan to Human Resources.

All training modules are accessible through the learning management
system. Contact training-support@meridian-ig.com for technical
issues or accommodation requests.

P. Hoffman
Director of Learning and Development
