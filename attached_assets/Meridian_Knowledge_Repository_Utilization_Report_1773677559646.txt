EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report provides utilization metrics for the Meridian Knowledge
Repository during Q4 2024. Total repository access increased 23
percent compared to Q3 2024, driven by the onboarding of 34 new
employees and the addition of 89 new documents to the repository.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Target the 75 employees who did not access the repository in Q4
   with targeted outreach and training to improve adoption.
2. Investigate high human override rate on AI classifications to
   identify systematic patterns for classifier improvement.
3. Reduce average approval time to under 2 business days by
   implementing the AI-assisted classification pilot in Q2 2025.
4. Schedule content review for documents not accessed in 12 months
   to identify candidates for retirement or update.
