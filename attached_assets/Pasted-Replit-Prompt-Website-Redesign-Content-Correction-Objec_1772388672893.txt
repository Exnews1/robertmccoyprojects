Replit Prompt: Website Redesign & Content Correction

Objective: Overhaul the robertmccoyprojects.com website. This involves two main tasks: a complete visual redesign to align with a new, more authoritative aesthetic, and a critical content update to correct outdated policy references across the site.




Part 1: Visual Redesign & Aesthetic Overhaul

Your Role: Act as a world-class web designer tasked with transforming a standard business website into a distinguished, authoritative platform for a senior-level government and aerospace consultant. The goal is to move away from the generic, light-themed "tech startup" look and adopt the gravitas of a formal, statutory document.

Core Inspiration: The aesthetic of the recently created CCME 2026 slide presentation. The design should feel structured, official, and serious.

Key Design Directives:

1.
Color Palette: Immediately replace the current light/blue theme with the following high-contrast, formal palette:

•
Primary Dark (for headers, footers, and accent panels): Dark Navy (#0F172A)

•
Primary Accent (for borders, links, and highlights): Antique Gold/Brown (#B45309)

•
Background: Off-White/Light Slate (#F8FAFC)

•
Body Text: Dark Slate (#334155)

•
Secondary Text/Borders: Medium Slate (#64748B)



2.
Typography: Implement a two-font system to create hierarchy and a sense of officialdom. Both fonts are available on Google Fonts.

•
Headings (h1, h2, h3): Use Merriweather (serif). This conveys a traditional, bookish authority.

•
Body Text (paragraphs, lists, links): Use Inter (sans-serif). This ensures maximum readability and a clean, modern feel for content.



3.
Layout & Structure:

•
Headers & Footers: Rebuild the main site header and footer to use the Dark Navy (#0F172A) background. Ensure text is white or a light accent color for contrast. Add a 4px Antique Gold (#B45309) top or bottom border to the header/footer to create a strong visual anchor, mimicking the slide design.

•
Buttons: Redesign all buttons. Primary call-to-action buttons should use the Dark Navy background with white text. Secondary buttons can be ghost buttons with an Antique Gold border and text.

•
Content Sections: Move away from generic, borderless cards. Define content sections with clear, sharp borders. Use left-borders (2-4px, Antique Gold) on key content blocks or blockquotes to draw attention and create structure.

•
Shadows & Rounded Corners: Drastically reduce or eliminate box-shadows and border-radius. The aesthetic is sharp, crisp, and flat, like a printed document. A subtle shadow on a key callout is acceptable, but the default should be no shadow.
