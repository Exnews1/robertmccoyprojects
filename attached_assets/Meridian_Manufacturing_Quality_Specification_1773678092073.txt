1. PURPOSE

This specification establishes the quality control standards,
inspection requirements, and acceptance criteria for all products
manufactured at Meridian Industrial Group facilities. All
manufacturing operations, inspection activities, and material
handling shall comply with these requirements.

2. INCOMING MATERIAL INSPECTION

All raw materials and purchased components shall undergo incoming
inspection before release to production. Inspection shall verify
material identity, dimensional conformance, and visual condition
against purchase order specifications. Sampling plans shall follow
ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 standards with Inspection Level II and an Acceptable
Quality Level of 1.0 percent.

Materials failing incoming inspection shall be quarantined in the
designated Material Review Board area. Disposition decisions
(return, rework, use-as-is with engineering approval) shall be
documented on the Nonconformance Report form.

3. IN-PROCESS QUALITY CONTROLS

3.1 Statistical Process Control
Critical manufacturing processes shall be monitored using
statistical process control charts. Control limits shall be set
at three standard deviations from the process mean. Processes
exhibiting out-of-control conditions shall be stopped immediately
and investigated before resuming production.

3.2 First Article Inspection
First article inspection shall be performed at the beginning of
each production run and after any process change. All dimensional
characteristics identified on the engineering drawing shall be
measured and recorded. Production shall not continue until first
article approval is obtained from the Quality Assurance Division.

4. FINAL INSPECTION AND TESTING

All finished products shall undergo final inspection before
shipment. Final inspection shall verify dimensional conformance,
functional performance, cosmetic appearance, and packaging
condition. Inspection records shall be retained for a minimum
of 10 years per the records retention policy.

5. CALIBRATION REQUIREMENTS

All measurement and test equipment used for quality inspection
shall be calibrated per MIG-SOP-EC-2024-019. Equipment with
expired calibration shall not be used for inspection purposes.
Out-of-tolerance conditions shall trigger an impact assessment
per the calibration SOP.
