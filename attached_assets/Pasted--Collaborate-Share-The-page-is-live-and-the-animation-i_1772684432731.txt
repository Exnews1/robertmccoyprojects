how do I record my screen
Straightforward answer based on what you are likely working on. You want to capture the signal flow animation running in fullscreen for use at CCME or on the site.
On a Mac
The built-in tool is QuickTime Player. Open it, go to File, then New Screen Recording. Click the record button, select the area of your screen or full screen, then play the animation. When done, stop the recording and it saves as an MP4. No software to install.
Alternatively, press Shift + Command + 5 simultaneously. A toolbar appears at the bottom of the screen with options to record the full screen or a selected portion. This is the fastest method.
On Windows
Press Windows + G to open the Xbox Game Bar. Click the record button (the circle) or press Windows + Alt + R to start and stop recording. The file saves to your Videos folder as an MP4.
Alternatively, if you have Windows 11, the Snipping Tool now includes screen recording. Search for it in the Start menu.
Practical recommendation for your use case
Go to the signal flow page, press fullscreen on the animation, then use your operating system's built-in recorder to capture the full 75-second run. That gives you a standalone MP4 you can embed in your CCME PowerPoint deck or upload to the site as a video asset. No third-party software needed.
