1. PURPOSE

This standard operating procedure establishes the requirements for
calibrating, verifying, and maintaining measurement and test
equipment used in manufacturing and quality control operations.

2. SCOPE

This procedure applies to all measurement and test equipment that
directly affects product quality, safety compliance, or regulatory
reporting, including dimensional gauges, pressure transducers,
temperature sensors, flow meters, and torque wrenches.

3. PROCEDURE

Step 1: Calibration Schedule Review
Review the calibration management database monthly to identify
equipment due within 30 days. Equipment past due shall be removed
from service immediately.

Step 2: Pre-Calibration Inspection
Inspect the equipment for physical damage, cleanliness, and proper
function. Document deficiencies on the Equipment Condition Report.

Step 3: Calibration Execution
Calibrate using reference standards traceable to NIST. Record all
data points, adjustments, and as-found versus as-left readings.

Step 4: Pass/Fail Determination
Compare results against tolerance specifications. Passing equipment
receives a calibration sticker. Failing equipment is quarantined.

Step 5: Documentation
Enter all records into the calibration database within one business
day. Retain records for a minimum of five years.

4. OUT-OF-TOLERANCE PROCEDURES

When equipment is found out of tolerance, evaluate all measurements
taken since the last successful calibration. If product quality may
have been affected, initiate a quality investigation.
