TO:       All Employees - Building C
FROM:     D. Park, VP of Operations

SUBJECT:  Building C Renovation and Temporary Relocation Plan

Document ID: MIG-MEM-OPS-Relocation-2025-025
Classification: Internal

------------------------------------------------------------------------

I am writing to provide advance notice that Building C will undergo
a comprehensive renovation beginning May 1, 2025. All teams
currently housed in Building C will be temporarily relocated to
Building A, Wing 3 for the duration of the renovation project,
estimated at 14 weeks.

RELOCATION TIMELINE

Phase 1 (April 14-18): IT infrastructure setup in Building A,
Wing 3. Network drops, phone systems, and printer stations will
be installed and tested.

Phase 2 (April 21-25): Furniture and equipment move. Facilities
will coordinate the physical transfer of desks, chairs, and
personal items. Employees should pack personal belongings using
the moving kits that will be distributed on April 14.

Phase 3 (April 28-30): Testing and settling. Employees report to
the new temporary locations and verify system access, phone lines,
and equipment functionality. IT support staff will be stationed
on-site throughout this period.

Phase 4 (May 1): Building C renovation begins. Access to Building C
will be restricted to authorized construction personnel only.

WHAT YOU NEED TO DO

  - Label all personal items and equipment with the provided tags
    by April 18
  - Back up all local files to the network drive before April 21
  - Update your desk phone forwarding to your mobile number during
    the transition weekend
  - Review the seating chart (posted to the intranet by April 7)
    and report any concerns to your manager

DURING RENOVATION

Building A, Wing 3 offers comparable meeting room capacity and
break room facilities. Parking assignments remain unchanged.
The employee shuttle route will be adjusted to include a stop
at the Wing 3 entrance.

Questions about the relocation should be directed to the
Facilities Help Desk at facilities@meridian-ig.com.

D. Park
VP of Operations
