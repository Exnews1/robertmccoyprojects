MODULE OVERVIEW

Welcome to Meridian Industrial Group. This safety orientation must
be completed before you begin work in any Meridian facility. The
module covers essential safety rules, hazard recognition, emergency
procedures, and your rights and responsibilities as an employee.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1: GENERAL SAFETY RULES

  - Wear required personal protective equipment for your work area
  - Report all injuries, illnesses, and near-misses immediately
  - Do not operate equipment without training and authorization
  - Keep work areas clean and free of trip hazards
  - Follow all posted signs, labels, and safety markings
  - Do not remove or disable safety guards or devices

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

PPE requirements vary by work area and task. At minimum, all
manufacturing floor employees must wear safety glasses and steel-
toed footwear. PPE is provided at no cost. Damaged PPE shall be
reported and replaced immediately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 3: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

Know the location of emergency exits, fire extinguishers, first
aid kits, and AEDs in your work area. When the fire alarm sounds,
evacuate immediately using the nearest safe exit and proceed to
your designated assembly point.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 4: HAZARD COMMUNICATION

Safety Data Sheets for all hazardous chemicals are available at
each work station and through the safety management portal. You
have the right to access SDS information at any time per OSHA
hazard communication requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 5: REPORTING AND YOUR RIGHTS

Report unsafe conditions to your supervisor or the Safety Division
at any time. You may report anonymously through the safety hotline.
No employee will face retaliation for reporting safety concerns.

Complete the Module 1 Assessment within 24 hours of orientation.
