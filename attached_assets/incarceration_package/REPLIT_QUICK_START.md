## Instructions for Replit Bot

### Project Goal
Build a professional, research-focused website presenting comprehensive findings on U.S. incarceration across four main sections:

1. **Economics of Incarceration**
2. **Life in Prison** (prisoners and guards)
3. **Education, Age, and Entry Dynamics**
4. **Prisoner Voice, Rights, and Mental Health**

## Critical Statistics to Highlight

### Homepage Hero Stats:
- **$39 billion** - Annual U.S. prison spending
- **790%** - Prison population growth (1980-2013)
- **44.3%** - Black prisoners (vs 13% general population)
- **66-76%** - Prisoners with mental health needs receiving NO treatment

### Section-Specific Key Numbers:
**Economics**: $29,291 per prisoner/year (federal average)  
**Prison Life**: 28 assaults per 1,000 inmates  
**Education**: 65% numeracy at/below 11-year-old level  
**Mental Health**: 16% have disorders, most untreated
