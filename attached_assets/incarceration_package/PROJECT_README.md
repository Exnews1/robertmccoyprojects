# U.S. Incarceration Research Project
## Understanding the Down and Dirty Reality of Incarcerated Life

**Edited by: Robert McCoy**

---

## Project Overview

This comprehensive research project examines United States correctional institutions through four critical lenses:

1. **Economics of Incarceration** - The financial costs and demographics
2. **Life in Prison** - Daily realities for prisoners and guards
3. **Education, Age, and Entry Dynamics** - Who enters prison and why
4. **Prisoner Voice, Rights, and Mental Health** - Human dignity and needs

### Research Foundation
- **728 unique scholarly papers** analyzed
- **40+ primary sources** cited in APA7 format
- **12,000+ word comprehensive report**
- **Focus**: Exclusively U.S. federal, state, county, and local correctional facilities

## Key Findings at a Glance

### Economics
- **$39 billion** annual U.S. prison spending
- **$29,291** average cost per federal prisoner
- **2-4x higher** costs for elderly prisoners
- **790% growth** in prison population (1980-2013)

### Racial Disparities
- **44.3%** of prisoners are Black (vs 13% of general population)
- **3.4x overrepresentation** of Black Americans
- **23%** Hispanic prisoners (vs 16% general population)

### Prison Life
- **<10%** of population controlled by gangs (despite perception)
- **28 assaults** per 1,000 inmates
- **70.1%** report retaliation after filing grievances

### Education
- **65%** have numeracy skills at/below 11-year-old level
- **48%** have reading skills at/below 11-year-old level
- **31.8 years** average age at admission

### Mental Health Crisis
- **16%** have diagnosed mental disorders (likely underreported)
- **66-76%** receive NO mental health treatment
- Elevated suicide risk in correctional facilities
