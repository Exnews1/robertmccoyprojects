1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the retention periods, storage requirements,
and disposition procedures for all organizational records maintained
by Meridian Industrial Group. The policy ensures compliance with
applicable federal, state, and industry-specific regulations.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all records created, received, or maintained
by employees and contractors, regardless of format. Records include
paper documents, electronic files, email, database entries, audio
and video recordings, and any other medium.

3. RETENTION SCHEDULE

Financial Records:
  - General ledger and journal entries: 7 years
  - Tax returns and supporting documentation: 7 years
  - Accounts payable and receivable: 5 years
  - Purchase orders and invoices: 5 years

Human Resources Records:
  - Employee personnel files: Duration of employment + 7 years
  - Payroll records: 7 years
  - Benefits enrollment: Duration of plan + 6 years
  - Workers compensation claims: 10 years from date of injury

Operational Records:
  - Contracts and agreements: Duration + 6 years
  - Insurance policies: Duration + 10 years
  - Quality assurance records: 10 years
  - Safety inspection reports: 5 years

Corporate Records:
  - Board meeting minutes: Permanent
  - Articles of incorporation: Permanent
  - Annual reports: Permanent
  - Audit reports: 7 years

4. LEGAL HOLD PROCEDURES

When litigation is reasonably anticipated, all affected records shall
be placed under legal hold. Recipients of a legal hold notice shall
suspend all routine disposition activities for affected records and
confirm compliance within 48 hours.

5. DISPOSITION

Records that have met their retention period and are not subject to
legal hold may be disposed of through approved methods. Paper records
shall be shredded using cross-cut equipment. Electronic records shall
be permanently deleted using approved data sanitization tools.
