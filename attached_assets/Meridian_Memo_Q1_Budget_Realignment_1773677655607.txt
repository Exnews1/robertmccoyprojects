Following the Q4 2024 financial review, the Executive Committee has
approved a budget realignment for Q1 2025 to address projected
revenue shortfalls in the commercial products division. This
memorandum outlines the required adjustments and spending controls
effective immediately.

BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS

All non-essential discretionary spending exceeding $5,000 requires
CFO approval through the budget exception request process. Travel
budgets are reduced by 20 percent across all divisions for Q1.
Hiring for non-critical positions is paused until further notice.
Capital expenditure requests exceeding $25,000 are deferred to Q2
pending revenue performance review.

PROTECTED CATEGORIES

The following budget categories are exempt from reductions: safety
and compliance expenditures, contractual obligations and existing
vendor commitments, employee compensation and benefits, and
customer-facing operations directly tied to revenue generation.

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Each department head shall submit a revised Q1 spending forecast
to the Finance Division by March 1, 2025. Monthly budget variance
reports will be reviewed at the Executive Committee meeting on
the second Tuesday of each month. Variances exceeding 10 percent
of the revised allocation require a written explanation.

The Finance Division is available to assist with reprioritization
and budget exception requests. Contact your assigned financial
analyst to schedule a planning session.

A. Reeves
Chief Financial Officer
