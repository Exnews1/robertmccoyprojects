Canonical Research → Operational Website

0. Build Mandate (Read First)

This site is an operational expansion of a canonical research body.
The research paper defines the system; the website demonstrates its behavior.

The system must:

Preserve individual agency

Enforce human decision authority

Prevent prescriptive or autonomous AI behavior

Scale in depth without redesign

Accept externally generated HTML artifacts over time

This is not a marketing site.
It is a governed system demonstration suitable for academic, policy, and institutional audiences.
