Translating Military Experience

Pillar 4 | 166 peer-reviewed sources (2015-2026)

Executive Summary

Veteran skills often translate imperfectly into civilian signals: employers vary in recognition and hiring outcomes, MOS-to-occupation crosswalks are uneven, and targeted bridging programs improve placement and wages.

Key Research Findings

Employer Hiring Outcomes

Veteran hiring reflects heterogeneous employer perceptions and conditional premiums for some veterans.

Barrier: Perceived nontransferability and terminology differences.

Employer Evidence Synthesis

  • Audit evidence: Employers give preferential callbacks to veterans in transferable roles but not combat-arms veterans (Kleykamp, 2013).
  • Decision factors: Applicant attributes, observer characteristics, job requirements all influence hiring (Stone & Stone, 2015).
  • HR perspectives: Challenges in resume translation and recognition of military training.

MOS Translation Barriers

Mapping MOS into civilian occupations uses several methodologies but faces taxonomy gaps.
