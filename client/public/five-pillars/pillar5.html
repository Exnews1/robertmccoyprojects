Evidence Synthesis

  • Population scale: More than 9,000 post-9/11 veterans followed; majority obtained postsecondary degree within four years (Rumann & Hamrick, 2010).
  • Institutional data: Enrollment patterns and aid sources predicted graduation and persistence.
  • Health impacts: Over 300,000 TBI cases (2000-2014) and high rates of PTSD symptoms documented (VA, 2018).

Barriers & Identity Conflicts

Interlocking institutional, financial, social, and psychological barriers while negotiating identity transitions.

Effective Supports & Outcomes

Dedicated veteran centers, cohort models, STEM scholarships, and peer networks show measurable improvements.

Cohorts VRCs Peer Networks

Intervention Evidence

  • Cohort models: Improved transition experiences and campus integration (DiRamio et al., 2008).
  • STEM scholarship cohort: 80% completion rate, +24 percentage points above institutional average (Morris et al., 2023).
  • Veteran resource centers: Associated with higher veteran success scores.
