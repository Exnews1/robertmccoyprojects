Framework Overview

The Career Mobility Governance Framework (CMGF) is a human-centered AI governance framework designed to improve the five pillars of support for the military education and transition system. This companion site synthesizes over 900 peer-reviewed sources to provide evidence-based foundations for each pillar.

Comprehensive Evidence Base

Over 900 peer-reviewed sources synthesized across five critical domains: career mobility outcomes, credentialing pathways, AI-assisted advising systems, military skills translation, and veteran student experiences.

Policy-Ready Analysis

Rigorous methodological critique, cross-pillar integration, and actionable recommendations designed for high-level government officials considering improvements to servicemember support systems.
