Empowerment Strategies & Stackable Pathways

Executive Summary

Military credentialing and stackable pathways show promise but uneven evidence: strong program-level successes exist (notably targeted PLA, competency-based pilots, and some apprenticeship outcomes), while national CA/COOL effectiveness and portable credential scale-up lack rigorous outcome evaluation.

Key Research Findings

Credentialing Programs

Targeted interventions report measurable successes, but national CA/COOL effectiveness lacks rigorous causal evaluation.

98% Pass Rate
Vets2BSN NCLEX

Program Effectiveness Analysis

  • Vets2BSN success: Nursing conversion project achieved 98% NCLEX pass rate among 49 completers (McNeal et al., 2019).
  • STEM scholarship model: 80% degree attainment, 24 percentage points above institutional average (Morris et al., 2023).
  • Insufficient national outcomes: Federal reviews note CA and COOL existence but lack causal evidence of improved employment outcomes (GAO, 2020).

Stackable Credentials & PLA

Prior learning assessment and competency-based education show plausible mechanisms for acceleration.

PLA CBE

PLA & CBE Research

  • Reservist Re-Entry pilot: Used learning-outcome mapping, tracked 350 students (Wainwright & Dhaliwal, 2012).
  • Colorado case study: Evaluated PLA implementation with system recommendations for military credit articulation (Deickman et al., 2024).
  • Sector outcomes: Strongest in health and STEM when aligned to licensure requirements.

Apprenticeship Effectiveness

Military apprenticeships (USMAP) account for 20% of all U.S. registered apprentices.

USMAP Analysis

Implementation evaluation finds potential to document competencies, but identifies weak participant understanding and poor employer engagement (Hanson & Lerman, 2016).

  • Incomplete documentation of military training
  • Inconsistent PLA practices across institutions
  • Limited employer engagement in hiring completers
