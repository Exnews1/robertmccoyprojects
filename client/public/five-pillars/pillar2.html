Empowerment Strategies & Stackable Pathways

Executive Summary

Military credentialing and stackable pathways show promise but uneven evidence: strong program-level successes exist (notably targeted PLA, competency-based pilots, and some apprenticeship outcomes), while national CA/COOL effectiveness and portable credential scale-up lack rigorous outcome evaluation.

Program Effectiveness Analysis

  • Insufficient national outcomes: Federal reviews note CA and COOL existence but lack causal evidence of improved employment.
  • Vets2BSN success: Nursing conversion project achieved 98% NCLEX pass rate among 49 completers.
  • STEM scholarship model: 80% degree attainment, 24 pp above institutional average.

Stackable Credentials & PLA

Prior learning assessment and competency-based education show plausible mechanisms for acceleration.

PLA & CBE Research

  • Reservist Re-Entry pilot: Used learning-outcome mapping, tracked 350 students.
  • Colorado case study: Evaluated PLA implementation with system recommendations.
  • Sector outcomes: Strongest in health and STEM when aligned to licensure.

Policy Recommendations

Priority Actions

  • Mandate rigorous outcome evaluation
  • Standardize PLA across institutions
  • Invest in digital credential registries
  • Engage employers in standards

Implementation

  • Outcome-based mapping frameworks
  • Employer-facing competency registries
  • Counselor training on mapping
  • Federal-state data coordination
