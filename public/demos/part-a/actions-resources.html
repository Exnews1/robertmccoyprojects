Potential Challenges

Shift Work Requirements

Airlines operate 24/7, may require night, weekend, and holiday shifts

Mitigation: Schedule flexibility improves with seniority; shift differential pay compensates

Industry Cyclicality

Aviation industry can be affected by economic downturns and fuel prices

Mitigation: Major carriers have strong union protection; maintenance is less volatile than other positions

Physical Demands

Job requires standing, climbing, and working in various weather conditions

Mitigation: Your military background provides good preparation; ergonomic equipment provided

Balancing Work & Education

Completing degree while working full-time requires time management

Mitigation: Part-time enrollment pace; online course options; employer support programs
