Financial Analysis Summary

Aviation Maintenance provides the strongest immediate financial position with no employment delay and immediate income generation. Over 5 years, this path generates approximately $58,000 more in net earnings compared to Aviation Management and $90,000 more than Technical Training. While Aviation Management offers higher long-term salary potential, the delayed employment and lost income during education significantly impacts the 5-year financial outcome.
