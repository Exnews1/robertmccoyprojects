AI Librarian Function

The AI mediation layer functions as a continuously available interpretive system that surfaces hidden constraints, structural conflicts, and alternative scenarios within complex institutional environments. It does not issue decisions, approvals, or predictions. Its purpose is to reduce unseen failure modes while preserving individual agency and human accountability.

AI Mediation Layer Architecture

Permitted Functions

Translation across taxonomies
Constraint checking
Explainability & traceability
Population-level aggregation

Prohibited Functions

Approve or deny requests
Rank or classify individuals
Predict individual outcomes
Trigger autonomous actions

Advisory Output Types

Constraint conflict alerts
Scenario comparisons
Missing information notices
Population trend indicators
All outputs clearly labeled as non-binding advisory signals
Human Authority
Final decisions
Action authorization
Policy interpretation

Governance Constraints

All AI outputs require human review
No direct system state alteration
No autonomous action initiation
All reasoning must be inspectable
